# SentinelAI benchmark

> Generated by `sentinelai evaluate`. **Synthetic data** produced by the project's own generator (`sentinelai/evaluation/generator.py`) - results show regression protection and the relative value of each component, *not* production accuracy. Real data will be harder.

- Transactions: **3000** (seed 7, regime `US_BSA`, 20.0% injected suspicious)
- Flag threshold: score >= **60**
- Throughput: **0.9 ms/transaction** (deterministic engine, single thread)
- Sanctions list: `SYNTHETIC_DEMO` (20 entries, synthetic demo data)

## Headline

Two operating points: **Review** (score >= medium threshold, the analyst queue) and **Escalate** (score >= high threshold). 45% of injected cases are *stealth* variants with deliberately weak/partial evidence, and ~55% of benign traffic is *hard negatives* (cash businesses, funded start-ups, new crypto users...).

| Method | Operating point | Precision | Recall | F1 | False-positive rate | ROC-AUC | Avg. precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Naive rules (>= $10k or any risky country) | fixed | 27.8% | 86.8% | 42.1% | 56.4% | 0.652 | - |
| Additive weights, clamped (previous scoring) | >= 30 | 61.5% | 99.5% | 76.0% | 15.6% | 0.961 | 0.784 |
| Additive weights, clamped (previous scoring) | >= 60 | 76.2% | 76.2% | 76.2% | 6.0% | 0.961 | 0.784 |
| **SentinelAI (noisy-OR)** | >= 30 | 64.8% | 99.2% | 78.4% | 13.5% | 0.963 | 0.832 |
| **SentinelAI (noisy-OR)** | >= 60 | 79.2% | 66.2% | 72.1% | 4.3% | 0.963 | 0.832 |

> The noisy-OR fusion is *comparable* to additive scoring on discrimination (AUC/AP) - its benefit is that the score saturates sensibly and decomposes **exactly** into per-signal contributions (explainability), not a large accuracy gain.

F1 95% bootstrap CI: 0.692 - 0.750; ROC-AUC 95% CI: 0.956 - 0.969; average precision: 0.832.
